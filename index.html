Where Strength and Softness Coexist.

Tula is the Sanskrit word for balance.

At Tula Movement & Wellness, we believe balance isn't about perfection. It's not a destination we achieve once, but a practice of returning to ourselves through the natural ebb and flow of life. Along the way, we learn to embrace both strength and softness, effort and ease.

Through yoga, Pilates, mindful movement, and wellness practices, we invite you to reconnect with yourself, move with intention, and return to balance again and again.
