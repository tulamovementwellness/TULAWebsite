More Than a Yoga Class

A practice that meets you exactly where you are and invites you to leave feeling stronger, calmer, and more connected than when you arrived.

At Tula, every class is thoughtfully designed with intention. My teaching blends creative, dynamic movement with mindful transitions and purposeful breath, creating a practice that flows naturally from beginning to end. Whether you're building strength in a flow class or finding stillness in a restorative practice, every class is an opportunity to cultivate balance in both body and mind.
