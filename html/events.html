How to Book

  1. Send an e-transfer to info@tulamovementwellness.ca and include your e-mail and the name of the class you're registering for in the message.
  2. Check your e-mail — once your payment is received, you will be sent a booking link to confirm your spot. Your spot is confirmed once you've completed the booking.
