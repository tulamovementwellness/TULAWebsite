Meet Krista

BA (Kinesiology), B.Ed, RYT 200, Mat Pilates

Hi, I'm Krista, founder of Tula Movement & Wellness.

I'm a passionate educator, movement teacher, and lifelong learner who believes movement has the power to transform how we connect with ourselves and the world around us.

I bring together my love of teaching and movement to create experiences that challenge both the body and the mind.

Inspired by the Sanskrit word Tula, meaning balance, I created Tula Movement & Wellness to be a space where strength and softness coexist and where movement becomes a way to reconnect with yourself.

My classes are creative, dynamic, and intentionally sequenced to guide you through a meaningful journey. Together, we'll build strength, explore movement with curiosity, and practice finding balance both on and off the mat.

My hope is that you'll leave feeling more grounded, more empowered, and more connected to yourself than when you arrived.

Because to me, movement isn't about perfection. It's about curiosity, self discovery, and learning to find balance through every season of life.
